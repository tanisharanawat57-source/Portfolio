// 01 — About

I grew up between cities, cantonments, and classrooms — an Army kid with a suitcase always half-packed and a notebook always full. That constant motion taught me to observe fast and feel deeply, and writing became the way I made sense of both.

I just finished Class 12 Commerce at Army Public School, Clement Town, and I'm heading into BBA (Business Analytics) at Chandigarh University. My writing lives at the intersection of raw feeling and clear thinking — poetry that confesses, essays that argue, scripts that land.

I've co-scripted and presented on Red FM 93.5 two seasons running, argued policy in Youth Parliament, and written about everything from dowry to the strange comfort of chaos. I'm looking for a content writing internship where I can bring all of that to real work.
